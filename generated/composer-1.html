Designer Finds Balance Between Craft and Curiosity

Special Correspondent

Hello, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I spend my days thinking about how tools can feel more intuitive and powerful. When I'm not at work, I tinker with side projects—shipflow, cursor-commands, and baby-notion—mostly because I can't help but wonder "what if we tried it this way?"

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. It's not meant to be impressive, just honest. Sometimes I'll share something I figured out, other times I'll just think out loud. The goal is to keep learning and stay curious.
