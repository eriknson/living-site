Product Designer Pioneers New Tools for the Future of Software

At Cursor, Erik Nilsson explores the frontier of AI-assisted development, building Shipflow and investigating mobile agent deployment.

Building shipflow and cursor-commands. Exploring ways to spin up agents from my phone. These are the projects consuming the winter days in Stockholm, where the cold sharpens focus and the abbreviated daylight creates natural rhythms for deep work.

As a Product Designer at Anysphere, the work centers on Cursor—described as the best way to design software with AI. The philosophy is one of simplicity and relentless iteration, refining every detail until the experience feels effortless.

The portfolio spans apps, websites, and brand identities for startups and global enterprises, reaching millions of users. Each project reflects a commitment to craft that transcends the purely functional, seeking the intersection where utility meets delight.
