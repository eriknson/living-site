Spring notebook, daily edition

Erik

Product design at Cursor. Quiet internet projects on the side.

Building tools that feel a little more human, and keeping a small corner of the web alive.

Most of my time goes into the editor at Cursor. Outside of work, many of my own commits land on living-site, an ongoing experiment in letting a personal site regenerate from everyday activity.

I live in Stockholm and spend my days thinking about product design, interfaces, and the little details that help people build with AI without making the whole thing feel louder than it needs to be. I like work that stays useful after the first impression wears off.

Lately that same instinct has been pulling me toward smaller side projects too: living-site, plus occasional pokes at terminal-design and shipflow. Nothing too grand. Mostly ways to keep learning in public and make a few ideas concrete.
