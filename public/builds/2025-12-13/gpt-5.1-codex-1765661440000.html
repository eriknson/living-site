Guiding approach

You set the vision, agents do the execution.
  • Set a plan and keep the core in focus.
  • Align every move to the vision before touching pixels.
  • Work with multiple agents to explore, compare, and refine.
  • Favor surgical iterations over sweeping one-shots.
  • Lock it in only once it clicks, then plan the next move.
