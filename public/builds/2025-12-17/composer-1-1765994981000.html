recent thoughts

shipping by sunrise has become a rhythm that works. there's something about early morning focus that cuts through the noise.

exploring ways to spin up agents from my phone. iOS shortcuts × github actions × cursor cli. the idea of building from anywhere, with parallel agents handling the execution, feels like the future.

best builds happen in steps. set a plan, align to your vision, focus on the core things. build with multiple agents, compare and see how it feels. iterate until it clicks. surgical moves over sweeping one shots.
