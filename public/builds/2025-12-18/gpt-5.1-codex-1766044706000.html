note 01 · studio pace

cursor-commands is my way of keeping the canvas quiet: script a shortcut, let it breathe, then test the same move inside cursor without leaving flow. i keep the prompts humble so the agent feels like a calm collaborator instead of a spectacle.

shipflow is where those instructions turn tactile. i'm aiming for point-and-click edits that feel trustworthy, so the interface stays almost invisible. activity dropped to twenty-four commits this phase versus my usual thirty-three on purpose: i want the gaps to tell me what to do next.

note 02 · field conditions

stockholm is overcast at four degrees, sunrise at 08:41 and sunset at 14:47. daylight is rationed, so i map the hardest thinking into the bright slice and leave reviews for the blue hour.

wind at eleven kilometers per hour keeps the air restless, but the apartment stays warm enough for long stretches at the desk. winter makes the notebook slower, which helps me listen.

note 04 · transmissions

i posted about "shipping by sunrise" and sketching agent triggers from my phone through shortcuts plus github actions. the pattern holds: set a plan, keep the scope narrow, compare models in parallel, then lock what feels right.
