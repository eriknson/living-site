From the Editor's Desk

Building the Future of Software Creation at Cursor

By @flowstated

There's a particular kind of focus that arrives with Stockholm's overcast winter skies—a soft, diffuse light that blankets the city and somehow sharpens the mind. It's in this environment that the work continues: making tools for building software with AI at Cursor.

The mission is deceptively simple in its articulation yet boundless in its implications. How do we reshape the very act of creation? How do we build instruments that amplify human intention rather than constrain it? These are the questions that animate the daily work.

Currently, the attention turns to a project called shipflow—point-and-click editing for Next.js applications, powered by Cursor Agent. The ambition is nothing less than making the invisible visible, the abstract tangible. Alongside it, cursor-commands promises one-click additions via the Cursor CLI, streamlining what once required ceremony and incantation.

Twenty-four commits this week mark the rhythm of progress—each one a small step toward a larger vision. The work is accompanied by a soundtrack as eclectic as the problems themselves: the dream pop swirls of Beach House, the neo-psychedelic landscapes of Tame Impala, the unexpected emotional clarity of Taylor Swift.

As winter settles over the city, the code continues to flow.
