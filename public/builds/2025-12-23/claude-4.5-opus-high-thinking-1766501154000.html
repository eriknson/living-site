Designer Crafts Tools for Building Software With AI at Cursor

From Stockholm, with quiet dedication

In the gentle hush of a Swedish winter, where the days are short and the northern light carries a particular clarity, one finds the ideal conditions for focused work. Erik Nilsson spends his days at Cursor, helping shape the future of how software is designed and built with artificial intelligence.

The work is the kind that requires patience—iterating on details until they feel just right, believing that simplicity is not the absence of complexity but its resolution. Over the years, this approach has touched apps, websites, and identities for both startups and global brands, reaching millions of users along the way.
