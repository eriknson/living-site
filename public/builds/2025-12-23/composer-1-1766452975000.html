Designer Builds Next-Generation Tools for AI-Assisted Software Development

By Erik Nilsson • Special to This Publication

STOCKHOLM — In the heart of Sweden's capital, a designer is quietly revolutionizing how developers interact with artificial intelligence. Erik Nilsson, a designer at Cursor, is building shipflow, an innovative point-and-click editing system for Next.js that leverages the power of Cursor Agent.

The project represents a new frontier in software development tools, combining intuitive design with cutting-edge AI capabilities. Alongside shipflow, Nilsson is also working on cursor-commands, further expanding the toolkit available to modern developers.
