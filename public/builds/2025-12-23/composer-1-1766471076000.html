Designer at Cursor Builds Tools for AI-Powered Software Development

Special to The Personal Times

STOCKHOLM — Erik Nilsson, a product designer at Anysphere, is building tools that revolutionize how software is designed and developed with artificial intelligence. Currently focused on Cursor, Nilsson believes in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels great. His approach combines technical understanding with aesthetic sensibility, creating tools that are both powerful and delightful to use.

With experience designing apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands reaching millions of users, Nilsson brings a refined approach to product design. His work emphasizes clarity, elegance, and thoughtful craftsmanship. Each project reflects a commitment to understanding user needs while pushing the boundaries of what's possible in digital design.

Current Projects: Shipflow and Open Source Contributions

Technology Desk

Nilsson is currently developing shipflow, a point-and-click editing system for Next.js applications, powered by Cursor Agent. This project represents a new approach to web development workflow, making complex editing tasks more accessible and intuitive. The system aims to bridge the gap between visual design and code, allowing designers and developers to work more seamlessly together.
