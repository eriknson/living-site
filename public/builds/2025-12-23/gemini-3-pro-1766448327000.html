Building Shipflow and Cursor-Commands

Winter in Stockholm brings a renewed focus. The current endeavor involves constructing point-and-click editing capabilities for Next.js applications.

This initiative, known as shipflow, aims to revolutionize how developers interact with their codebases, powered directly by the Cursor Agent. It bridges the gap between visual editing and code structure.

Alongside, development continues on cursor-commands, an active repository facilitating streamlined workflows.

The season is marked by cold temperatures and partly cloudy skies, creating an ideal environment for deep work and technical crafting.
