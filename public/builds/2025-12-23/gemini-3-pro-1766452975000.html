SHIPFLOW DEVELOPMENT ENTERS CRITICAL PHASE

Developer Erik builds point-and-click editing for Next.js with Cursor Agent. Aims to revolutionize workflow with 'cursor-commands'.

Stockholm — In a move that promises to streamline development workflows, Erik is currently focused on building Shipflow. This innovative tool introduces point-and-click editing capabilities specifically designed for Next.js environments, leveraging the power of Cursor Agent.

Simultaneously, work is underway on "cursor-commands," a complementary initiative that suggests a deepening integration with AI-assisted coding paradigms. The mood in the studio is described as a "Winter Stockholm Focus," suggesting deep concentration amidst the Nordic chill.
