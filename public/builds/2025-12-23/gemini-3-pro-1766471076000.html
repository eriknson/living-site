Featured Project

Building shipflow

"Point-and-click editing for Next.js, powered by Cursor Agent."

In a move that redefines the developer experience, Erik is currently focused on shipflow. This innovative tool promises to bring point-and-click editing capabilities to Next.js applications, leveraging the power of the Cursor Agent. It represents a shift towards more intuitive, visual software development.

"I'm building Cursor, the best way to design software with AI," says Erik, a Product Designer at Anysphere. His philosophy centers on simplicity and relentless iteration until every detail feels great. Having designed apps and identities for startups and global brands, Erik brings a seasoned eye to this new endeavor.

The mood in Stockholm is one of focused winter energy. With temperatures hovering around freezing, the environment is perfect for deep work.
