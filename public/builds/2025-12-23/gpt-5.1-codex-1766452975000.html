Winter Dispatch

Stockholm trades daylight for focus this time of year, and the work follows suit. Shipflow is getting the polish it deserves—tiny typographic affordances, more confident inline editors, guardrails that still feel human. Cursor Commands is the companion notebook, letting me carve shortcuts into the agent so teams can point, click, and just get on with it.

Coffee steam curls over the monitor while I map tactile gestures into pixels. The prototype reads like an old broadsheet: stacked columns of context, accusations of precision, the occasional wink. The goal is clear—make AI tooling feel edited, not engineered.

Field Notes

Cursor-era interaction design is a balancing act: let the agent sprint while the designer keeps the prose tight. I am rewriting how Next.js builders edit layouts—direct manipulation, no dusty control panels, just tangible handles right on top of the work.

Cursor Commands keeps evolving. Each new verb is a newsroom style correction applied to the product: shorter steps, brighter outcomes, fewer excuses. Expect new composable routines soon.

When the snow hits the Gamla Stan rooftops, I trade code for sketchbooks and write the next cues for the agent to follow.
