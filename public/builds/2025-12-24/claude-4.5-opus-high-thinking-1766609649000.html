Designer Finds Joy in the Craft of Building

From quiet mornings in Stockholm to shipping products that matter

Hej, I'm Erik. These days, I spend my time on the design team at Cursor, working on tools that help people write code. It's the kind of work that reminds me why I fell in love with design in the first place—making complex things feel simple.

Before this, I had the privilege of designing and building apps, websites, and identities for teams at Metamask, Consensys, Accenture, and Miltton. Each project taught me something new about the intersection of aesthetics and utility.
