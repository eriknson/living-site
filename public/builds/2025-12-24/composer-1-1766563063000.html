Building Tools for the Future, One Day at a Time

By Erik Nilsson ❄ Special to The Daily Dispatch

In the quiet of a Stockholm winter, where the days are short and the focus is long, work continues on tools that make building software feel more like crafting than coding. The current project: shipflow — point-and-click editing for Next.js, powered by Cursor Agent.

There's something quietly satisfying about building tools that help others build. It's not about grand gestures or loud announcements. It's about the small details, the moments when everything clicks, when the flow feels just right. That's where the real work happens, in those quiet spaces between the code.

Product design at Anysphere means believing in simplicity, iterating until every detail feels great. It means designing apps, websites, and identities that reach millions, but doing it with the same care you'd give to something personal. That's the approach: quiet confidence, steady progress, attention to what matters.
