Building the Future of Design Tools

By Erik Nilsson — Stockholm, Sweden

Hej, I'm Erik.

I work on the design team at Cursor, where we're building the best way to design software with AI. Currently, I'm focused on shipflow—point-and-click editing for Next.js powered by Cursor Agent—and working on cursor-commands, a GitHub repository that's pushing the boundaries of what's possible.

Previously, I designed and built apps, websites, and identities for global brands at Metamask, Consensys, Accenture, and Miltton. I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels great.
