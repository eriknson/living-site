Designer Builds Revolutionary Tools for AI-Powered Development

By Erik Nilsson • Special to The Daily Erik

Hej, I'm Erik. I work on the design team at Cursor, where we're building the best way to design software with AI. As a Product Designer at Cursor by Anysphere, I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels great.

Previously, I've designed and built apps, websites, and identities for global brands at Metamask, Consensys, Accenture, and Miltton. My approach centers on making building software easier and more fun, with AI agents as collaborators rather than replacements.

Currently, I'm focused on shipflow—point-and-click editing for Next.js, powered by Cursor Agent—and cursor-commands, a collection of Cursor commands. This week alone, I've made 24 commits across these projects, maintaining a steady pace of focused development.

My philosophy is simple: stay in flow, never leave context. I prefer surgical iterations over sweeping rewrites, and I believe in vision-driven development where you steer and agents execute. Building software has never been this fun.
