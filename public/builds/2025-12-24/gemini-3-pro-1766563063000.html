Shipflow Sets Sail: A New Approach to Next.js Development

Building point-and-click editing tools powered by Cursor Agent, aiming to simplify the software design process.

Erik is building Cursor, seeking the best way to design software with AI. Currently serving as a Product Designer at Anysphere, he operates with a belief in simplicity and the relentless iteration required until every detail feels great. His portfolio includes apps, websites, and identities for both startups and global brands, reaching millions of users.

This winter finds him in Stockholm, focused on "shipflow"—a tool designed to bring point-and-click editing to Next.js projects. Alongside this, he maintains "cursor-commands," a repository of templates for the very tool helping him build. The mood is quiet, focused, and festive.
