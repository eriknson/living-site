Building Software Has Never Been This Fun

By Erik • Product Designer

I'm currently a Product Designer at Anysphere, where we are building Cursor, the best way to design software with AI. I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels great.

Lately, my focus has been on Shipflow, a point-and-click editing tool for Next.js powered by Cursor Agent. It's about moving between high-level ideas and tiny surgical edits without ever leaving your flow state.

The vision is clear: AI agents as collaborators, not replacements. You steer, they execute.
