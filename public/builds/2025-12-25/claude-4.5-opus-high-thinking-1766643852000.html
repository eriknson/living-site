Swedish Designer Continues Work at Cursor Through Winter Season

Building tools for developers while exploring mobile-first agent workflows from Stockholm

From the Desk of Erik Nilsson

Hej, I'm Erik. I work on the design team at Cursor, where I help shape how developers interact with AI-powered coding tools. These days I'm focused on building cursor-commands and shipflow—exploring ways to spin up agents directly from my phone.

Previously, I designed and built apps, websites, and identities for global brands. My work has taken me through the halls of Metamask, Consensys, Accenture, and Miltton—each stop adding new perspectives on how design and technology intersect.
