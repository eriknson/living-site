From the desk of Erik

Hello, I'm Erik. I work as a product designer at Cursor, where we're building what we hope becomes the best way to create software with AI. I'm from Sweden, and I believe good design happens when you keep iterating until every small detail feels just right. Before this, I spent my time designing apps, websites, and identities—some for startups finding their way, others for larger companies with established names.

Right now I'm especially focused on shipflow, a project exploring point-and-click editing for Next.js applications powered by Cursor Agent. The idea is simple: what if you could edit your app by just clicking on what you want to change? I'm also working on cursor-commands, making it easier to add commands with a single click.

This site is something of a playground—a place to try things and eventually share what I learn. It rebuilds itself automatically, which keeps me honest about shipping rather than perfecting endlessly in the dark.
