✦ Designer Builds AI Tools from Stockholm Apartment ✦

Special Christmas Edition — Reporting from the Design Team at Cursor

STOCKHOLM — Erik Nilsson, a product designer working on the design team at Cursor, continues development on cursor-commands and shipflow, a point-and-click editing system for Next.js applications powered by Cursor Agent.

Nilsson reports exploring methods to spin up agents directly from his phone, indicating ongoing experimentation with mobile-first development workflows. The projects represent part of his current focus during the winter months in Stockholm.

Previously, Nilsson designed and built applications, websites, and brand identities for global organizations including MetaMask, Consensys, Accenture, and Miltton. His work emphasizes simplicity and iterative refinement until details meet his standards.
