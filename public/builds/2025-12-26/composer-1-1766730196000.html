Building Tools for the Future of Design

A quiet update from a designer working in Stockholm

Hello, I'm Erik. I'm a product designer from Sweden, currently building shipflow—point-and-click editing for Next.js, powered by Cursor Agent. It's been a journey of learning and iteration, trying to make something that feels right.

I'm also working on cursor-commands, a GitHub repository for Cursor command configurations. These are small projects, but they represent the kind of work I find meaningful: tools that make the creative process a bit smoother.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. It rebuilds itself with Cursor CLI, which feels fitting for someone who spends their days thinking about how we build things.
