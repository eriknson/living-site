Product Designer Crafts Tools at Cursor

Exploring the intersection of design, code, and AI agents.
BY ERIK NILSSON

Stockholm — Amidst the winter chill, the focus remains sharp. I am a product designer based in Sweden, currently shaping the future of coding at Cursor. The work is a blend of rigorous design systems and experimental tinkering, finding ways to make software development more intuitive.

This digital space serves as both a portfolio and a playground—a "living site" that rebuilds itself. It is where I document my learnings, try new things, and write about the process.
