Designing tools with a humble winter focus.

I'm Erik, working on Cursor—helping build thoughtful AI tooling so teams can ship faster. Quiet evenings go to shipflow and cursor-commands, two experiments in making creative momentum feel less fragile.

Notebook

Present Focus

Cursor is in a season of refining the craft of AI pair programming. I'm designing interaction patterns that feel trustworthy, generous, and respectful of the craft people bring to their editor.

Side projects stay small on purpose: shipflow is a scratchpad for learning loops, and cursor-commands is a utility belt for anyone curious about automating creative chores.

Working Principles

  • Make tools that feel like a calm collaborator, not a spectacle.
  • Prefer small steady improvements over loud launches.
  • Write things down so the next build starts warm.
  • Keep the interface humble—let the work carry the signal.
