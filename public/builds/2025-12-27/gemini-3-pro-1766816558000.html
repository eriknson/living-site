Product Designer Crafts Tools for Intelligence

Exploring the intersection of human creativity and artificial reasoning at Cursor.

Hello, I'm Erik. I am a Product Designer at Cursor, where we are dedicating our days to making tools for building software with AI. It is an endeavor that requires both precision and imagination.

When I am not in the studio, I can be found tinkering with side projects such as cursor-commands and shipflow. This site is my personal playground—a digital broadsheet to try new things and chronicle what I learn.
