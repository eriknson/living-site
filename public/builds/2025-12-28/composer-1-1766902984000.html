Designer Finds Purpose in Building Tools for the Future

By Erik Nilsson

Hello, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I help make tools for building software with AI. It's work I care about—trying to figure out how to make complex things feel simple, and iterating until the details feel right.

When I'm not at Cursor, I tinker with side projects like cursor-commands and shipflow. These are small experiments, ways to try things and learn. Nothing groundbreaking, just things that interest me.

Previously, I've designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands. But honestly, the work I'm doing now feels most meaningful—building tools that might help other people build things.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. It's always changing, always a work in progress. Like most things worth doing.
