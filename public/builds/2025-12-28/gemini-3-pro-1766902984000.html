Product Designer Shapes Tools for the AI Age

By ERIK NILSSON • STOCKHOLM BUREAU

In a world increasingly driven by automated intelligence, the role of human craftsmanship remains paramount. As a Product Designer at Cursor, the mission is clear: building tools that empower developers to build software with AI.

From the quiet corners of Stockholm, amidst the winter chill, work continues on refining the interaction between human intent and machine capability. When not designing the future of coding at Cursor, tinkering persists on side projects like cursor-commands and shipflow.
