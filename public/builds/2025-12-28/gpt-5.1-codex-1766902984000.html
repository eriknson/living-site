Tools that disappear into the work

The Cursor team is deep in the humble pursuit of shipping AI-native workflows that feel obvious once you touch them. It's winter outside, so the focus tilts toward warmth: fewer menus, clearer intent, and commands that keep pace with a builder’s thought process.

Cursor-commands lives as a personal proving ground—where Siri shortcuts spark GitHub Actions, and agents wake up from a phone before sunrise. Shipflow, meanwhile, is an ongoing attempt to make “shipping by sunrise” feel less like a metaphor and more like a reliable cadence.
