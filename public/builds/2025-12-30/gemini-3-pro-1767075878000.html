Designing the Future of Software

Product Designer at Cursor reflects on simplicity, craft, and the winter focus in Stockholm.

STOCKHOLM — Winter has settled over the city, bringing with it a quiet focus. I am currently designing Cursor, making tools for building software with AI. It is a time for tinkering and deep work.

I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels great. Previously, I've designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands. Now, my days are filled with the isometric intricacies of city building and the future of coding.
