Designer Builds Tools for AI Future;
Tinkers with Digital Cities

Erik is a Product Designer at Cursor, where he spends his days crafting the tools that will define how software is built in the age of Artificial Intelligence. In a world increasingly defined by speed, he maintains a belief in simplicity and the art of iteration.

When not designing the future of coding, Erik can be found tinkering with isometric-city, a digital playground that reflects his passion for detailed, systemic design. His work has previously spanned apps, websites, and identities for both scrappy startups and established global brands.

Currently situated in Stockholm, the mood is described as "quiet winter," conducive to deep work and reflection.
