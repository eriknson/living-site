A Quiet Winter Letter from the Cursor Desk

Product Designer at Cursor, quietly making tools so builders and agents can ship before sunrise. On the side, I keep tinkering with an isometric city that looks better every week I remember to nurture it.

Stockholm is calm this week. The workday is measured in deep focus, not fanfare. I like it that way: fewer lights, more clarity. Three commits landed in isometric-city, each one a small nudge toward a model town I want to wander through someday.

Cursor keeps me honest. Designing for people who build with AI means no shortcuts. Every interaction has to hold up in bright offices and gloomy kitchens alike, on laptops that are barely awake. If you ended up here, thanks for reading — this site is my way of keeping the edge sharp.
