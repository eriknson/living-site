A Designer Tinkering at the Edge of What's Possible

On building software with care, one detail at a time

Hello, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I spend my days trying to make software design feel a little more human. This site is my small corner of the internet — a place to try things out and write about what I learn along the way.

I believe in simplicity and in iterating until every detail feels right. Before Cursor, I designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands. But really, I'm just someone who likes making things and occasionally writing about the process.
