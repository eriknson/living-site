Building the Future at Cursor

Product Designer tinkering with isometric-city in a quiet, snowy Stockholm.

Hello, I'm Erik. This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. Currently, I am focused on crafting tools for the future of coding as a Product Designer at Cursor.

Outside of my main work, I'm deep in the process of building isometric-city and exploring new ways to spin up AI agents directly from my phone using iOS shortcuts, GitHub Actions, and the Cursor CLI.

It's a quiet winter here in Stockholm, the perfect atmosphere for deep work and surgical edits to the things that matter.
