Product Designer Finds Balance in Code and Craft

By Erik Nilsson | Special to This Edition

Hej, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I get to shape tools that help people build better things. When I'm not designing, I'm usually tinkering with side projects—cursor-web, living-site, and shipflow keep me busy these days.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. It's not meant to impress anyone, just a quiet corner of the web where I can experiment and share what feels worth sharing.

I've been thinking a lot about how websites can feel more alive, more responsive to the world around them. That's led me down some interesting paths—exploring how CI can become a runtime, how agents can maintain sites autonomously, and how small experiments can teach big lessons.
