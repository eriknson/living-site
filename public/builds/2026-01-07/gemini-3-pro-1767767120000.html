Product Designer Finds Focus in Winter Chill

Hej, I'm Erik. Currently serving as a Product Designer at Cursor, I spend my days tinkering with the intersection of design and code. My current focus is on building tools like cursor-web, living-site, and shipflow.

The winter in Stockholm brings a quiet focus, allowing for deep dives into product mechanics and interface craft. I treat this space as a playground—a digital broadsheet to document experiments and share what I learn along the way.
