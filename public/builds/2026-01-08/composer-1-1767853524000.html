Product Designer Finds Joy in Building Tools

A quiet morning in Stockholm, overcast skies above

Hej, I'm Erik. I work at Cursor, where I help design tools for building software with AI. It's work I find meaningful—taking complex ideas and making them feel simple and delightful.

When I'm not at work, I tinker with a few side projects. Nothing too serious, just things that interest me and help me learn. I've been working on cursor-web (a web interface for managing Cursor Cloud Agents), living-site (this self-regenerating website you're reading), and shipflow (point-and-click editing for Next.js).
