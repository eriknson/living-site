Product Designer Tinkers at Cursor

Exploring the intersection of design, code, and AI agents in the deep winter.

By ERIK NILSSON

Hej, I'm Erik. Currently serving as a Product Designer at Cursor, I spend my days (and often nights) tinkering with living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. While the Stockholm winter settles in with its characteristic chill, the digital workspace remains vibrant.

This site serves as my personal playground—a digital broadsheet, if you will—to experiment with new ideas and document my learnings. Whether it's spinning up agents from my phone or refining the craft of software, the goal remains the same: to build things that feel alive.
