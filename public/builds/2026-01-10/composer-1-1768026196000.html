Building Things, Learning Along the Way

A brief dispatch from the workshop

Hej, I'm Erik. This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I get to help shape how people design software with AI. It's work I care about deeply, though I try not to take myself too seriously.

Outside of that, I tinker with a few side projects—living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. They're small experiments, really. Nothing groundbreaking, just things that interest me and help me learn. I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels right, even if it takes a while to get there.

Previously, I've designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands. Each project taught me something new, and I'm grateful for those experiences. But I'm more interested in what's next than what's behind.

Currently

Working on a few things: living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. Mostly just exploring ideas and seeing where they lead.

Listening

A mix of Swedish pop, classic rock, dream pop, and whatever else feels right. Dire Straits, Beach House, Ben Böhmer, Arctic Monkeys, and plenty more. Music helps me think.
