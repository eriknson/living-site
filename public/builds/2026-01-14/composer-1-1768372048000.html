Designing with intention, one detail at a time

A brief dispatch from the studio

Hej, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I get to help shape tools that make building software feel more natural. It's work I care about deeply, though I try not to take myself too seriously.

When I'm not at Cursor, I tinker with a few side projects—living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. They're small experiments, really. Places to try things and see what sticks. Nothing revolutionary, just honest attempts to learn and build something useful.

This site is my playground. A space to write about what I'm learning, share thoughts that feel worth sharing, and occasionally document the process. If any of that sounds interesting, you can follow along on X, check out my GitHub, or send me an email.
