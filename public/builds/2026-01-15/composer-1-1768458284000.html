Building Tools, Learning Along the Way

A brief note from a designer tinkering in Stockholm

Hej, I'm Erik.

I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I help shape how people design software with AI. It's work I care about deeply—finding the right balance between powerful capabilities and thoughtful simplicity.

Outside of that, I tinker. Small projects mostly—cursor-web, living-site, shipflow. Nothing groundbreaking, just experiments that help me learn. This site itself is one of those experiments, a place to try things and occasionally write about what I discover.

I believe good design comes from iteration and attention to detail. Not perfectionism, but the willingness to keep refining until things feel right. Sometimes that means shipping something simple and improving it over time. Other times it means taking a step back and reconsidering the approach entirely.

If any of this resonates, feel free to reach out. You can find me on X, check out my code on GitHub, or send an email.
