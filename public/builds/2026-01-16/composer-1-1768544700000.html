Designing Tools and Tinkering with Ideas

By Erik Nilsson

Hej, I'm Erik.

I work as a product designer at Cursor, where I help build tools for making software. Outside of work, I tinker with side projects—cursor-web, living-site, shipflow—small experiments that keep me learning.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. It's a living thing, always changing, always growing. Sometimes I write about design, sometimes about code, sometimes just about the process of making things.

I try to keep things simple and iterate until details feel right. I've designed apps, websites, and identities for various companies over the years, but these days I'm mostly focused on making tools that feel good to use.
