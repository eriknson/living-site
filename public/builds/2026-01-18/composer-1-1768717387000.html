Designer Finds Focus in Stockholm's Winter Light

A quiet reflection on building, listening, and the craft of making things

Hej, I'm Erik. This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I spend my days thinking about how to make software design feel effortless and delightful. On the side, I tinker with small projects like living-site and cursor-web—experiments that help me understand what I'm curious about.

These days, Stockholm wears its winter coat well. The overcast skies and crisp air create a kind of focus that's hard to find in brighter seasons. It's the kind of weather that makes you want to stay inside, make something, and listen to music that matches the mood.

This week, I've been listening to a mix of Beach House, Taylor Swift, Kanye West, and Tame Impala—dream pop, rap, and indie sounds that somehow all fit together when the world outside is gray. Music has a way of shaping the work, or maybe it's the other way around.

I've been quiet on Twitter lately, but that's okay. Sometimes the best work happens when you're not broadcasting every thought. Thirty-eight commits this week across a few small projects—nothing groundbreaking, just steady progress on things that matter to me.

If you'd like to follow along, you can find me on X, check out my GitHub, or send me an email. I'm always curious to hear what others are building.

This site itself is one of those experiments—a living thing that changes with the seasons and reflects what I'm thinking about. Right now, it's dressed up like a winter newspaper, because that's what feels right for January in Stockholm.
