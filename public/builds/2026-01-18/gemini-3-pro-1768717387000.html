Product Designer Tinkers with Living Site at Cursor

By Erik Nilsson • Stockholm Bureau

Stockholm — In a quiet corner of the internet, a Product Designer at Cursor is reshaping his digital presence. Amidst the overcast skies of Stockholm, Erik Nilsson has been observed tinkering with a new "living-site" and exploring the frontiers of web development with "cursor-web" on the side.

The project represents a shift towards more personal, handcrafted digital spaces. "Hej," he says simply, summarizing the humble approach to his work. While the winter chill settles over the city at 3°C, the digital activity remains high with 38 commits logged this week alone.
