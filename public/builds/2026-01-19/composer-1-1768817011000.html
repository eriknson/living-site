Product Designer Finds Balance Between Work and Side Projects

By Erik Nilsson

Hej, I'm Erik.

I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I help build tools for creating software with AI. It's work I find meaningful—watching people use what we make to bring their ideas to life.

When I'm not at Cursor, I tinker. There's something satisfying about building things just to see if they work. Right now I'm playing with living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. Nothing revolutionary, just experiments that sometimes turn into something useful.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. Follow me on X, checkout my GitHub, or send me an email.
