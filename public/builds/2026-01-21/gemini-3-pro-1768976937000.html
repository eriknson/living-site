Designer Tinkers with "Living Site" Concept

STOCKHOLM — In a quiet corner of the digital landscape, Product Designer Erik Nilsson continues to refine his craft. Currently deployed at Cursor, he believes in simplicity and the power of iterating until every detail feels correct. His latest experiment, a "living website," attempts to bridge the gap between static portfolios and the fluid nature of daily work.

"I'm exploring ways to spin up agents from my phone," he noted in a recent dispatch. The intersection of iOS shortcuts, GitHub actions, and CLI tools seems to be his current area of focus, aiming for a workflow where surgical edits replace sweeping rewrites.
