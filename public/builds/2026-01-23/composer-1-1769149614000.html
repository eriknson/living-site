A Brief Introduction

By Erik Nilsson

Hej, I'm Erik.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I get to help shape tools that make building software a bit more delightful. When I'm not working, I'm usually tinkering with side projects—living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow keep me busy these days.

I believe in keeping things simple and honest. There's something beautiful about straightforward design and genuine communication. If any of this resonates with you, feel free to follow along on X, check out my GitHub, or send me an email.
