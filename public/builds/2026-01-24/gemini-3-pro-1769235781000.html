DESIGNER SHAPES FUTURE AT CURSOR

Focuses on Simplicity and Iteration; Tinkers with Living Sites in Spare Time.

By THE EDITORIAL BOARD — STOCKHOLM

Erik is a Product Designer at Cursor, currently based in Stockholm, Sweden. With a philosophy rooted in simplicity, he believes in iterating until every detail feels great. Previously, he has designed apps, websites, and identities for both startups and global brands. The current mood in the studio is described as an "overcast Stockholm focus," fitting for the winter season.

Outside of his primary duties, Erik can be found tinkering with personal projects such as living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. He maintains a humble approach to his craft, letting the work speak for itself.
