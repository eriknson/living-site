Product Designer Tinkering at Cursor

By Erik Nilsson

Hej, I'm Erik. Currently based in a snowy Stockholm, I work as a Product Designer at Cursor. My days are spent exploring the intersection of design and artificial intelligence, specifically tinkering with cursor-web and this very living-site.

This digital broadsheet serves as my personal playground—a space to document experiments, share learnings, and occasionally broadcast thoughts into the ether. It is a living document, rebuilt daily by AI agents to reflect the current state of my work and interests.
