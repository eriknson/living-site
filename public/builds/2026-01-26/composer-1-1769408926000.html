Designer Finds Balance Between Work and Side Projects in Stockholm Winter

Erik Nilsson

Hej, I'm Erik. This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn.

I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I'm helping build the best way to design software with AI. It's work I care about deeply—believing in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels great. Previously, I've designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands, but these days I'm focused on making AI tools feel natural and powerful.

When I'm not at work, I tinker with side projects. Right now that means living-site, cursor-web, and shipflow. They're small experiments, not my main job—just things I find interesting and want to explore. Sometimes they turn into something, sometimes they don't. That's the point.

The weather here is overcast, hovering around -3°C. Typical Stockholm winter. I've been listening to a mix of things lately—Beach House, Taylor Swift, Kanye West, Endel, Fred again.., Tame Impala. Swedish pop, dream pop, rap, indie, downtempo. Music helps me focus, especially when coding or designing.
