Winter Focus in Stockholm

Hej. I am a Product Designer at Cursor, currently embracing the dark, cold days of a Stockholm winter to focus on building. There is a quiet clarity that comes with the season here. The snow dampens the sound of the city, and the limited daylight turns the screen into a hearth.

Lately, I've been tinkering with the concept of a "living site" — a digital space that breathes and evolves with its creator. This very page is an experiment in that direction, rebuilt by agents to reflect what I'm up to. I believe in simplicity, iterating until every detail feels inevitable, and crafting software that feels human.
