Building tools for the future of design

A brief dispatch from a designer tinkering in Stockholm

Hej, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where we're building the best way to design software with AI. When I'm not working, I tinker with small projects like living-site and cursor-web—experiments that help me learn and stay curious.

I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels right. Previously, I've designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands, but these days I'm focused on making tools that help people create better things.
