Designing in the Dark: A Winter in Stockholm

Product Designer at Cursor explores the intersection of AI tools and personal craftsmanship while listening to Swedish Pop.

Hej, I'm Erik. Currently serving as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I believe in the power of simplicity and the relentless iteration required until every detail feels absolute. The winter in Stockholm is dark, but the screens are bright. I spend my days building the best way to design software with AI, and my nights tinkering with living digital artifacts.

This site serves as a playground—a digital broadsheet where I can test new ideas, write about what I learn, and catalogue the small shifts in technology that capture my attention. It is a work in progress, much like the seasons outside.
