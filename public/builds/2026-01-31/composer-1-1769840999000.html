Product Designer Finds Solace in Code Working on tools for building software with AI

By Erik Nilsson • Special to The Personal Dispatch

Hej, I'm Erik. This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where we're building tools that help people create software with AI. It's work that feels meaningful—taking complex ideas and making them feel simple and right.

Outside of work, I tinker with small projects. There's cursor-web, a web interface for Cursor Cloud Agents that I've been exploring. And there's living-site, this very website you're reading, which regenerates itself—a small experiment in what a personal site can be when it's not static.

The winter here in Stockholm is clear and crisp. The kind that makes you want to stay inside and build things. I've been listening to a mix of Swedish pop, melodic house, and dream pop—Jacub, A Camp, kent, Beach House, Ben Böhmer. Music that fits the mood of these short days.
