Building Tools for the AI Era
Product Designer at Cursor redefines how software is made.

STOCKHOLM — Hej, I'm Erik. Currently serving as a Product Designer at Cursor, where the intersection of human creativity and artificial intelligence is being mapped daily. My work focuses on simplicity, iterating until every detail feels right, and ensuring that tools empower rather than replace the builder.

In the quiet cold of a Swedish winter, I am tinkering with new concepts: a self-regenerating living site and web interfaces for cloud agents.
