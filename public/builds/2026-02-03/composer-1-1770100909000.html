Designer Finds Balance Between Work and Side Projects

By Erik Nilsson — Special to The Personal Dispatch

Hej, I'm Erik.

I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I help build tools that make designing software with AI feel natural and powerful. It's work I care about, and I'm grateful to be part of it.

When I'm not at work, I tinker with small projects. Right now that means working on living-site and cursor-web—experiments that help me learn and stay curious. They're just side projects, nothing fancy, but they keep me engaged.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. I believe in simplicity and iterating until every detail feels right. Previously, I've designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands, but these days I'm focused on making things that feel genuinely useful.
