Building The Future at Cursor

A Product Designer's journey through code and craft.

I am a Product Designer at Cursor, currently based in Stockholm. My philosophy is grounded in the belief that simplicity is the ultimate sophistication. I spend my days tinkering with the intersection of design and artificial intelligence, striving to create tools that feel not just functional, but magical.

Previously, I have designed apps, websites, and identities for startups and global brands. Today, my focus is singular: iterating on details until they feel absolutely right. When I am not pushing pixels or code, I am likely exploring new music or working on my "living site" project.
