Erik Nilsson: Designing the Future at Cursor

By THE EDITORIAL BOARD

STOCKHOLM — In the quiet focus of a Scandinavian winter, Erik Nilsson continues his work as a Product Designer at Cursor. Known for a thoughtful approach to digital craftsmanship, he spends his days tinkering with the boundaries of web interfaces and self-regenerating sites.

Far from the noise of the industry, his current focus lies in the subtle art of "living sites" — digital spaces that rebuild themselves daily based on activity data — and the robust architecture of cursor-web, a new interface for launching and managing Cloud Agents.
