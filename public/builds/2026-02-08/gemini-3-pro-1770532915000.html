Product Designer at Cursor

Tinkering with living sites and exploring the future of software creation.
By Erik Nilsson — Stockholm Bureau

Hej, I'm Erik. This site serves as my personal playground—a digital broadsheet where I experiment with new ideas and document what I learn along the way. In a world of rapid feeds and fleeting updates, I'm looking to craft something a bit more enduring.

Currently, I am working as a Product Designer at Cursor, where we are building the next generation of coding tools. When I'm not designing, I'm likely tinkering with my "living-site" project or exploring the intersection of design and engineering.
