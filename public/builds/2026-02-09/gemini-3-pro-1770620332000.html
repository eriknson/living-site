Building the Future of Software with AI at Cursor

I am a Product Designer at Cursor, where we are actively crafting tools that redefine how software is built using artificial intelligence. The work involves a deep dive into the intersection of human creativity and machine capability.

When not in the studio, I can be found tinkering with a living-site, a side project exploring digital permanence and evolution. My focus currently rests on the Stockholm winter, embracing the cold while designing warm, functional interfaces.
