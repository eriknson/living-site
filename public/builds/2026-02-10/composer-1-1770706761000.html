Product Designer at Cursor

Hej, I'm Erik. I work as a Product Designer at Cursor, where I help build tools for creating software with AI. It's interesting work—figuring out how to make complex systems feel simple and approachable.

When I'm not at Cursor, I'm tinkering with a side project called living-site. It's a small experiment in making websites that feel more alive, more connected to the moment. Still figuring out what that means exactly, but that's part of the fun.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. Nothing too polished, just honest thoughts and experiments.
