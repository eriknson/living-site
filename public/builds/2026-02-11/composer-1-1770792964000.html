Designer Finds Joy in Building Things That Build Themselves

By Erik Nilsson • Product Designer at Cursor

Hej, I'm Erik. I spend my days designing products at Cursor, where I get to work on tools that help people build better things. It's work I care about, and I'm grateful to be doing it.

When I'm not at work, I'm usually tinkering with what I call a "living-site" — a personal website that regenerates itself. It's a small experiment in making something that feels alive, that changes with the seasons and reflects what I'm actually up to. Nothing fancy, just a way to keep things interesting.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. I don't claim to have all the answers, but I enjoy sharing what I discover along the way. If any of it resonates with you, feel free to follow along on X, check out my GitHub, or send me an email.
