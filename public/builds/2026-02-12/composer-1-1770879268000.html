A brief introduction from the editor

Hej, I'm Erik.

Product Designer at Cursor, currently tinkering with what I'm calling a "living-site" — a self-regenerating personal website that updates itself. It's been an interesting experiment in letting code write code, and seeing what emerges.

This site is my playground to try things and write about what I learn. Nothing too serious, just thoughts and experiments as they come. Follow me on X, checkout my GitHub, or send me an email.

Most of my time these days goes to a few things:

  • shipflow — Point-and-click editing for Next.js. Still early, but the idea is to make shipping faster.

Nothing revolutionary, just trying to solve problems I bump into day to day.
