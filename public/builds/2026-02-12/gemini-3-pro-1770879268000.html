Product Designer Tinkers at Cursor

In the quiet overcast of Stockholm, Erik Nilsson continues his work as a Product Designer at Cursor. Between the hours of deep focus, he has been observed tinkering with the concept of a "living-site"—a digital space that breathes and evolves. It is a humble pursuit of craft in a digital age.

Currently, his attention is divided among three primary endeavors: the aforementioned living-site (a self-regenerating personal website), cursor-web (a web interface for Cursor Cloud Agents), and shipflow (point-and-click editing for Next.js).

Cultural Notes

The studio is currently filled with the sounds of Kent, Endel, Dr. Dre, A Camp, Beach House, Tame Impala, and Taylor Swift.
