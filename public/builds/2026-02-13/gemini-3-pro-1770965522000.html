Product Designer Joins Cursor, Continues Web Experiments

By ERIK NILSSON — STOCKHOLM

Hej, I'm Erik. Currently serving as a Product Designer at Cursor, I spend my days bridging the gap between human intent and machine execution. When the sun sets over the archipelago, I turn my attention to living-site, an ongoing experiment in self-regenerating personal web presence.

This digital broadsheet serves as my playground to test new ideas, break things, and occasionally write about what I learn in the process. It is a humble attempt to reclaim the craftsmanship of the early web, wrapped in the comfort of yesterday's news.
