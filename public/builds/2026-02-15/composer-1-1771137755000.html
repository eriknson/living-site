Hej, I'm Erik

Stockholm, Sweden

I work as a product designer at Cursor, trying to figure out how to make tools that actually help people build things. It's a good gig—I get to think about design problems all day, which is pretty much what I'd be doing anyway.

When I'm not working, I'm usually tinkering with side projects. Most of them never see the light of day, but that's fine. The process is what matters. Right now I'm playing around with this idea of a "living site"—something that changes and grows over time, rather than staying static.

This site is my place to try things and write about what I learn. It's not polished or perfect, and I'm okay with that. Sometimes I'll share thoughts on design, sometimes on code, sometimes on nothing in particular. Mostly it's just me figuring things out as I go.
