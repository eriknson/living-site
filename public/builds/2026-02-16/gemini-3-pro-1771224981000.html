Cover Story

The State of the Living Site

"I'm exploring the intersection of design, code, and AI agents to create self-regenerating web experiences."

As a Product Designer at Cursor, my work often revolves around the cutting edge of developer tools. But in the quiet hours of the Swedish winter, I turn my attention to a more personal experiment: the Living Site.

The concept is simple yet profound. A website that isn't just a static brochure, but a dynamic entity that evolves. Currently, I am deeply focused on living-site, a self-regenerating personal website project, alongside building cursor-web, a web interface for Cursor Cloud Agents.

When not deep in code, I'm working on shipflow, bringing point-and-click editing capabilities to Next.js, bridging the gap between visual design and rigorous engineering.
