Product Designer Tinkers with Digital Crafts

Exploring the intersection of design, engineering, and the spaces in between.

HSTOCKHOLM — Hej, I'm Erik. This site serves as my personal playground—a digital workshop where I experiment with new ideas and document my learning process. In an era of rapid technological shifts, I find solace in the craft of building.

Currently, I am working as a Product Designer at Cursor, where we are reimagining how software is built. When not designing tools for developers, I spend my time tinkering with side projects like living-site and plugin-template.

My work sits at the intersection of design and engineering, always striving for that sweet spot where utility meets delight.
