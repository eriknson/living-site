A Quiet Introduction

Hej, I am Erik. Most days I design products with the Cursor team, then spend evenings tinkering on a project called living-site. It is a small experiment where AI agents rebuild this site each day from activity data, mostly to see what happens when a personal site becomes a living process instead of a static page.

I try to keep the work practical and honest: fewer big declarations, more consistent steps. Some days that means shipping, and some days that means deleting drafts that were not ready.
