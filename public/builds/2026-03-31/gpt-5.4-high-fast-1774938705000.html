Hej, I'm Erik. I design products by trying to remove noise until the important part has room to breathe. At Cursor that means design for AI-native developer tools. Away from work, I keep a handful of side projects close by so I can keep learning without pretending every experiment needs a grand story. Lately the main one is living-site, a page that regenerates itself from everyday signals and gives me a place to mix design, code, and routine.

Nothing here is meant to be louder than it needs to be. The goal is just honest work, careful details, and a site that feels looked after.
