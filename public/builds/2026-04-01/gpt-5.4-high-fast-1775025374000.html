About

Hej, I am Erik. I work on product design at Cursor, usually trying to make complicated tools feel calm, legible, and a little easier to trust.

Outside work I keep a few personal projects moving at a patient pace. Lately that mostly means living-site, where agents rebuild this page from daily data, with the odd push to shipflow or terminal-design.

I like software that feels considered before it feels loud. Most of the work is in details: alignment, rhythm, wording, and the small decisions that help something hold together over time.
