About

I'm Erik, a product designer at Cursor. Most of my work is about making software feel clearer, calmer, and a little more obvious in use. Outside work I tend to make small things in HTML, follow ideas longer than they probably deserve, and write now and then when something sticks.

Right now that mostly means poking at living-site, tinkering with terminal-design and shipflow when I get a minute, and keeping this page simple enough that it can change as life does. Today is cool and overcast in San Francisco, which feels like the right weather for careful work.
