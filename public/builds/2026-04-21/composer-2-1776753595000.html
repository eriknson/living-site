About

I work in product design at Cursor. Most days that means pixels, flows, and the fuzzy space between what a tool promises and what it actually feels like to use.

On the side I keep tinkering with living-site — a small HTML experiment that rebuilds itself from daily activity. It is half diary, half excuse to learn how agents and automation can shape a site without turning it into a billboard.

This page is another playground: loud color, quiet copy. If something looks broken, it is probably me learning in public.
