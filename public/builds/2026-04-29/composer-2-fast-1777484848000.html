This week's vending-machine idea

You're the router: swipe across three forks to shepherd glowing requests before the upstream queue corrupts. Horizontal swipe swaps lanes; wrong lanes spawn ghost duplicates that munch your buffer. Each round clears a wave; a clean route feels like a deploy that actually stuck. Traffic thickens until the motorway becomes a braid — at which point we recommend closing the laptop and touching grass, but nobody does.
