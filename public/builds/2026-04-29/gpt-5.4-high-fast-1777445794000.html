One Wild Motif

Game direction for today

Beach-town fog. One distant light. Trust your memory.

Today's side sketch imagines a tiny traveler moving through fog by a path you draw once and then have to remember after the ink fades. The goal is simple enough: reach the warm beacon before the ring closes. Naturally, it stops being simple the second the shoreline starts drifting.

Mechanic: draw-and-remember pathfinding. Touch control: drag to sketch the route, release to lock the next steps. Aesthetic: soft watercolor mist, one distant sodium light, and just enough pressure to keep it honest.
