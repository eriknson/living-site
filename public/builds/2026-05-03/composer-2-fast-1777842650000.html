Field notes

I’m a product designer at Cursor. Most days I’m sketching flows, debating edge cases, and trying not to confuse “good enough to ship” with “done pretending we care.”

Off-hours I’m messing with living-site experiments — little agents rebuilding this pocket of the internet from whatever happened that week. It’s imperfect on purpose; the goal is freshness, not a keynote slide.
