Current work

Mostly trying to make powerful things feel calm.

My day job is product design at Cursor. The work is less about making AI look flashy and more about helping people stay oriented while the tools get strange, fast, and genuinely useful.

Outside of work, the GitHub repos are side projects and experiments. Little personal things. Some become useful, some teach me what not to do, and some are just a way to keep touching the material.
