Designing around the person at the keyboard.

I work on Cursor, where the interesting part is not only what AI can do, but how it should feel when it is sitting next to someone trying to make something real.

I like tools that leave room for taste, hesitation, weird half-ideas, and the quiet part of craft. The best interfaces do not make a person feel replaced. They help them stay in the work a little longer.

Outside the job, GitHub is mostly small personal experiments. Some of them are useful. Some are just a way to keep my hands moving.
