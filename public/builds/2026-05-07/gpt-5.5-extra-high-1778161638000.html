Product designer at Cursor, working on how people build software with AI. This page is less a portfolio and more a daily field note with a strange interface attached.

I like tools that feel calm under pressure: things that make room for taste, iteration, and the messy middle of making software. Most days that means designing workflows where agents can help without taking the steering wheel away.

Off-hours, I tinker with this living-site experiment. It gets rebuilt from fresh activity data, then leaves a trace of the day: fog, music, half-formed ideas, and whatever little systems I was curious about.
