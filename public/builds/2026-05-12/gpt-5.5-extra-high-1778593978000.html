About the work

I like software when it gives you leverage without making you perform for it.

Most of my work is the less flashy part of product design: shaping flows, reducing friction, making states legible, and iterating until the thing feels calm in your hands.

At Cursor, that means designing for people building with AI agents. The interesting part is not the magic trick. It is how much control, context, and confidence a person can keep while the system gets more capable.

Small operating notes
