Notes from the desk

My day job is figuring out the small, human moments inside an AI editor — the kind where help should feel like a thoughtful colleague, not a vending machine. It's a strange, good problem.

The side I tinker on most is something I've been calling living-site: a personal web page that quietly rewrites itself from fresh activity each morning. The version you're reading was generated a few hours ago. Tomorrow it'll be a different one.

This week has been unusually quiet on the public side — no commits, no posts, fog at the windows. Sometimes the harbor is empty on purpose.
