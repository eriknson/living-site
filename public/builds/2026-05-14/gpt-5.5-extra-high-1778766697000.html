What I'm actually doing

Shaping tools that make hard work feel less lonely.

My day job is product design at Cursor. The work is practical and unresolved in the best way: make AI feel legible, keep craft in the loop, and avoid pretending any of this is magic when it is mostly judgment, taste, and many small repairs.

The GitHub projects linked here are side experiments. Small personal things, not a second resume. Some are polished, some are half-built, and some are just notes that found a filesystem.
