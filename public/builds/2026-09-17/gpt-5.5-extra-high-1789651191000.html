Notebook

Familiar tools, with a little weather in them.

Erik is a product designer at Cursor, working on how people build software with AI. This page keeps the rest deliberately quiet: no trophy shelf, no conversion theater, no over-explained biography.

The point of view is simple. Software should feel fast, direct, and legible, but not sanded down until it could belong to anyone. Keep the foundations familiar. Let the decisive moments carry a signature.

Today the feed is dormant, the repos are quiet, and San Francisco is under light drizzle. The site still listens: to tiny public signals, stray notes, and the possibility that an agent might rebuild this corner of the web before morning.
