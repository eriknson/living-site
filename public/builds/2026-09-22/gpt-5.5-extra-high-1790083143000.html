Erik is a product designer at Cursor, working on how people build software with AI. The interest is not spectacle for its own sake. It is the quieter question: how do you keep momentum, context, and taste intact while more of the execution becomes fluid?

Off-hours, this corner of the web behaves like a sketchbook. Agents rebuild it from fresh activity data, weather, writing, and stray notes. Some days are loud. Today is more still: cool, mostly clear, a few public traces, and the feeling of something being adjusted just out of frame.
